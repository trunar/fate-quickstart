## Usage

Gear in Fate is an **Extra** – something narratively significant enough to have mechanical weight. Most items don't need stats. If you can pick up any sword off a rack, it doesn't need an aspect. If the sword has a name, a history, or does something no other sword does – it does. Usually, gear aspects and stunts occupy your standard slots. However, if your campaign involves frequent loot or gear swaps, you might allow these extras to exist outside your normal limits – perhaps one per gear type.

Three patterns are used here, in increasing complexity. Use the simplest one that fits.

---

## Aspect Only

The item exists, matters narratively, and can be invoked or compelled. No extra rules. Use this for most story-important gear.

**How it works:** Write the item as an aspect on your character sheet. Invoke it when relevant, accept compels when it causes trouble.

---

### The Grandfather's Revolver

_An old service weapon, worn smooth, never cleaned but never jammed._

**Aspect** – _My Grandfather Carried This at the End of the World_

Invoke when the history or reliability of the weapon matters. Compel when sentiment overrides good judgment – you won't leave it behind, won't trade it, won't let someone else use it.

---

### The Stolen Signet Ring

_Belongs to someone powerful. You have it for reasons you'd rather not explain._

**Aspect** – _The Ring That Opens Doors and Closes Throats_

Invoke when impersonating, gaining access, or leveraging the associated name. Compel when the real owner, their allies, or anyone who recognizes it makes your life complicated.

---

### The Dead Mentor's Coat

_Heavy, patched, smells like tobacco and old decisions._

**Aspect** – _She Wore This When She Was Unstoppable_

Invoke when drawing on legacy, reputation, or sheer stubbornness. Compel when it marks you as her successor to people who hated her.

---

### The Cursed Map

_Shows the way to something. The something is not safe. The map may not be accurate._

**Aspect** – _The Map That's Always Right and Always Wrong_

Invoke when navigating, investigating, or making a case that you know where you're going. Compel when the map leads somewhere worse than expected or attracts people who want it.

---

### The Alien Shard

_Smooth, warm, hums at frequencies you feel in your back teeth._

**Aspect** – _Something Old Enough to Have Watched Stars Die_

Invoke when its age, strangeness, or unknowable properties are useful. Compel when it attracts attention from people or things that recognize what it is.

---

## Aspect and Stunt

The item has narrative weight and a reliable mechanical effect. Use this for weapons, tools, or items with a consistent special property.

**How it works:** The aspect works as above. The stunt activates under specific conditions – usually once per scene or with a skill requirement.

---

### Ironveil Armor

_Plates of layered black steel, old craft, heavier than it looks._

**Aspect** – _Proof Against Everything Except Regret_

**Stunt** – _Ironveil_ – Once per conflict, reduce a physical consequence by one step by letting the armor take it instead; afterward, the aspect _Dented and Compromised_ is placed on the armor until repaired.

---

### The Sharpshooter's Longrifle

_Custom-built, hand-balanced, accurate past the point of reason._

**Aspect** – _Built for One Shot That Ends Arguments_

**Stunt** – _Breath and Trigger_ – When I take a full action to aim before shooting, my next Shoot attack gets +2 and ignores any single aspect that would penalize the shot (cover, range, weather).

---

### The Surgeon's Kit

_Old leather case, everything in its place, nothing wasted._

**Aspect** – _The Right Tool for Whatever Is Wrong with You_

**Stunt** – _Proper Equipment_ – +2 to Lore or Crafts when treating injuries, illness, or performing procedures; once per scene, clear a mild physical consequence from a patient without a roll if I have time and quiet.

---

### The Lockbreaker

_A device – mechanical, magical, or electronic depending on the setting – that shouldn't work as well as it does._

**Aspect** – _If It Locks, It Opens_

**Stunt** – _Master Key_ – +2 to Burglary or Crafts when bypassing physical locks, security mechanisms, or sealed systems; on success with style, I leave no evidence of entry.

---

### The Bound Blade

_Forged with something inside it. It has opinions._

**Aspect** – _The Blade Remembers Every Kill It's Made_

**Stunt** – _Blood Memory_ – When I attack a type of creature or person the blade has harmed before (established in play or at character creation), I get +2 to Fight; when I refuse a kill the blade wants, accept a compel or take a mild mental consequence from the resistance.

---

### The Diplomat's Seal

_Official. Heavy. Opens mouths that should stay shut._

**Aspect** – _Authority Stamped in Wax and Fear_

**Stunt** – _By Order Of_ – Once per session, invoke the seal's authority to compel a minor NPC to cooperate, stand down, or grant access without a roll; this doesn't work on anyone with reason to doubt the authority it represents.

---

### The Exosuit Gauntlets

_Hydraulic-assisted, cracked along one knuckle, loud when you flex._

**Aspect** – _These Hands Have Punched Through Walls_

**Stunt** – _Augmented Force_ – +2 to Physique when breaking, lifting, or striking objects; Fight attacks made with the gauntlets deal one additional stress on a success (not success with style).

---

### The Seeing Glass

_A lens – monocle, scope, or implant – that shows more than it should._

**Aspect** – _I See What You're Hiding_

**Stunt** – _True Sight_ – Once per scene, use the glass to ask the GM one question about a person, object, or location in my line of sight; they must answer honestly.

---

## Aspect, Stunt, and Boxes

The item has narrative weight, a mechanical effect, and a limited resource – charges, durability, ammunition, or uses before something changes. When the boxes are gone, something happens: the item breaks, runs out, needs recharging, or transforms.

**How it works:** Boxes are checked off as used. Define at creation what happens when the last box is checked.

---

### The Healer's Phials

_Six vials of something clear and faintly warm. There were more once._

**Aspect** – _One Dose Left Is One More Than Nothing_

**Stunt** – _Field Medicine_ – Spend a phial (check one box) to immediately clear a mild physical consequence on yourself or someone you can touch; no roll required.

**Uses** □□□□□□ _When all boxes are checked: the phials are empty. They can be refilled with the right resources and a Crafts or Lore roll at the end of a session._

---

### The Overcharged Pistol

_Modified past what the manufacturer intended. Hits harder. Wears out faster._

**Aspect** – _Dangerous to Everyone in the Room, Especially Me_

**Stunt** – _Overclock_ – When you fire on overcharge (check one box), the attack deals two additional stress on a hit; on a miss, something goes wrong – the GM decides what.

**Charges** □□□□ _When all boxes are checked: the pistol is burnt out and useless until repaired (Crafts roll, one session downtime)._

---

### The Warding Stone

_Carved with something old. Warm to the touch. Getting cooler._

**Aspect** – _The Old Powers Still Owe Me a Few Favors_

**Stunt** – _Ward_ – Spend a charge (check one box) to negate one magical, supernatural, or psychic attack against yourself or someone adjacent; the attack simply does not land.

**Charges** □□□ _When all boxes are checked: the stone is cold and dark. It may be recharged at a place of power, through a ritual, or at significant narrative cost._

---

### The Blacksite Keycard

_Cloned access credentials. Every use risks detection._

**Aspect** – _Opens the Doors That Shouldn't Open_

**Stunt** – _Unauthorized Access_ – Use the keycard to bypass any electronic security door, system, or checkpoint without a roll; check one box each use.

**Uses** □□□□□ _When all boxes are checked: the credentials have been flagged. Using the card again automatically triggers an alarm and likely a response._

---

### The Ticking Grenade

_Whatever kind fits your setting. The point is it goes away._

**Aspect** – _One Answer to Several Problems at Once_

**Stunt** – _Blast_ – Throw the grenade (check one box) to attack everyone in a zone at Fight or Shoot +2; on success, also place a scene aspect on the zone with one free invocation.

**Grenades** □□□ _When all boxes are checked: you're out. Resupply requires resources, contacts, or a relevant overcome roll._

---

### The Dying Familiar

_A companion, construct, or bound spirit. Still here. Not for much longer._

**Aspect** – _Still With Me, Against All Reason_

**Stunt** – _Last Reserves_ – Once per scene, the familiar intervenes – taking a hit meant for you, providing a critical piece of information, or creating a boost through sacrifice; check one box.

**Remaining** □□□ _When all boxes are checked: the familiar is gone. This should be a scene, not a footnote. The aspect becomes_ Already Gone _and can still be invoked – as grief, as legacy, as memory._

---

## Notes on Building Your Own

**Aspect only** – ask: does this item have a history, a reputation, or a complication? If yes, it gets an aspect. If it's just a tool, it doesn't need one.

**Add a stunt when** the item does something consistently special that no skill alone covers – a bonus under specific conditions, a permission to do something otherwise impossible, or a once-per-scene effect.

**Add boxes when** the item is consumable, fragile, or has a countdown built into its fiction. Define what happens at zero before play begins – broken, empty, transformed, or lost.

**Cost and permission** – consider what it takes to have the item at all. Some gear requires a skill rating, a faction affiliation, a specific aspect, or a narrative justification. That cost is part of what makes it feel significant.
