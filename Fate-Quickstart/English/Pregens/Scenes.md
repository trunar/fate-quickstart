## Usage

Each scene template is a tactical archetype – rename the zones, reskin the fiction, drop it into any setting.

Each entry has:

- **Zones** – distinct areas with their own aspect; movement between adjacent zones is free, non-adjacent costs an action or a roll
- **Scene Aspects** – permanent aspects on the whole scene, available to everyone
- **Hazards** – active elements that act on their own; each has a skill and difficulty for when they trigger
- **Complication** – something that changes mid-scene or that players may not notice until it matters

---

## Combat Scenes

---

### The Crossfire

_A space with no good cover, contested from multiple sides. Being still is the most dangerous thing._

**Zones**

|Zone|Aspect|
|---|---|
|The Open Ground|_Nowhere to Hide_|
|The Rubble Line|_Low Cover, High Risk_|
|The Flank Approach|_Out of Sight, Out of Support_|
|The Chokepoint|_One Way In, One Way Out_|
|The High Position|_Commanding View, Obvious Target_|

**Scene Aspects**

- _Visibility Is a Liability_
- _Every Position Has a Counter-Position_

**Hazards**

- _Suppressing Fire_ – at the start of each exchange, anyone in the Open Ground who didn't move there voluntarily must defend against Shoot at +3 or take a boost their attacker can use immediately
- _Unstable Flank_ – the Flank Approach has a crumbling or unreliable edge; moving through it requires an Athletics overcome at +2 or gain the aspect _Off-Balance_

**Complication** A third party – not allied to either side – arrives partway through and has their own agenda. They may shoot at everyone, try to extract something, or simply complicate the geometry.

---

### The Defensive Hold

_One side is dug in. The other is trying to get through. Time favors the attackers._

**Zones**

|Zone|Aspect|
|---|---|
|The Perimeter|_First Line, Fragile_|
|The Killzone|_Open Ground Between Them and Us_|
|The Barricade|_Solid Cover, Limited Sightlines_|
|The Interior|_Protected but Trapped_|
|The Breach|_Where the Wall Is Weakest_|

**Scene Aspects**

- _The Defenders Know Every Corner_
- _The Clock Is Running Against the Held Position_

**Hazards**

- _The Breach_ – at the end of each exchange in which no one has acted to reinforce it, the Breach widens; after three exchanges unaddressed, attackers may enter the Interior without passing through the Barricade
- _Collateral Damage_ – each exchange that involves an attack in the Interior, roll Shoot or Fight at +2 against a randomly determined piece of the environment; on a hit, a scene aspect is destroyed or changed

**Complication** Something inside the Interior needs protecting and cannot defend itself – a person, an object, a mechanism that keeps the whole position viable. It becomes a target.

---

### The Duel

_Two people, or two small groups, with nothing between them but intent. The environment matters because there is nothing else._

**Zones**

|Zone|Aspect|
|---|---|
|Your Ground|_Known Footing, Familiar Reach_|
|The Middle|_Committed, No Easy Retreat_|
|Their Ground|_Their Advantage, Your Audacity_|
|The Edge|_One Mistake Away from Out_|

**Scene Aspects**

- _Everyone Is Watching_
- _Honor or Pride Has Made This Necessary_

**Hazards**

- _The Edge_ – anyone pushed into the Edge zone must make an Athletics overcome at +3 or be taken out of the scene by environmental means (a fall, a boundary, a crowd) rather than their opponent
- _The Audience_ – if a combatant invokes _Everyone Is Watching_ against an opponent, the opponent must either accept the compel or spend a fate point; if they accept, they must act in a way that maintains their reputation even at tactical cost

**Complication** Someone in the watching crowd has a reason for the duel to end a specific way – and acts on it before the end.

---

### The Running Fight

_Combat while moving through a space. Neither side has a fixed position. The environment is a weapon._

**Zones**

|Zone|Aspect|
|---|---|
|The Lead|_First Through, Fewest Obstacles_|
|The Tight Passage|_Single File, No Flanking_|
|The Obstacle Field|_Broken Ground, Scattered Cover_|
|The Drop|_Lower Ground, Harder to Retreat From_|
|The Exit|_Close Enough to Want, Far Enough to Die For_|

**Scene Aspects**

- _The Environment Does Not Care Who Wins_
- _Speed Is the Only Defense_

**Hazards**

- _The Tight Passage_ – anyone fighting in this zone takes –1 to all attack rolls due to restricted movement; on any attack that succeeds with style here, the attacker may instead choose to block the passage entirely for one exchange
- _The Obstacle Field_ – moving through this zone costs an Athletics overcome at +2; failure means the character arrives _Sprawled and Exposed_, a boost their nearest enemy may use immediately

**Complication** The exit closes – a door, a gate, a collapse, a crowd. Now the fight has a new objective layered on top of the original one.

## Chase Scenes

---

### The Urban Chase

_Streets, alleys, rooftops, crowds. The city is a maze and only one party knows it well._

**Zones**

|Zone|Aspect|
|---|---|
|The Main Street|_Witnesses Everywhere, Nothing Moves Fast_|
|The Alley Network|_Shortcuts and Dead Ends_|
|The Market Crowd|_Dense, Loud, Impossible to See Through_|
|The Rooftops|_Fast If You Don't Fall_|
|The Service Passage|_Known Only to Those Who Work Here_|

**Scene Aspects**

- _The City Belongs to Whoever Knows It_
- _Everyone Here Has Seen Nothing and Will Say So_

**Hazards**

- _The Market Crowd_ – anyone moving through at speed must overcome with Athletics at +3; failure creates the aspect _Caused a Scene_ with a free invocation for whoever is pursuing
- _The Rooftops_ – each exchange spent here, roll Notice at +2; failure means a gap or edge that wasn't visible; take a mild physical consequence or lose a zone of distance

**Complication** A third party – uninvolved, panicked, or simply unlucky – gets pulled into the chase and becomes an obstacle or an accidental witness.

---

### The Vehicle Chase

_Two or more vehicles, open ground or structured route, speed as the primary resource._

**Zones**

|Zone|Aspect|
|---|---|
|Far Ahead|_Out of Range, Barely in Sight_|
|Closing Distance|_In Range, Not in Control_|
|Side by Side|_Everything Is Personal Now_|
|Falling Behind|_Losing Ground, Losing Options_|
|The Hazard Stretch|_The Road Is the Enemy Too_|

**Scene Aspects**

- _Speed Forgives Nothing_
- _The Gap Between Vehicles Is a Weapon_

**Hazards**

- _The Hazard Stretch_ – each vehicle passing through must overcome with Drive or Pilot at +3; failure means a collision, a spin, or a forced zone shift and the aspect _Damaged and Struggling_
- _Ranged Attacks from Vehicles_ – all Shoot attacks made from a moving vehicle take –1 unless the shooter has a stunt or aspect justifying otherwise

**Complication** The route changes – a road closes, a bridge is out, the destination moves. Both parties must adapt mid-chase.

---

### The Crowd Escape

_One person or group trying to vanish into a mass of people. The pursuers have authority or numbers. The environment is the only advantage._

**Zones**

| Zone              | Aspect                              |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| The Thick of It   | _Invisible in the Press of Bodies_  |
| The Thinning Edge | _Easier to Move, Easier to Spot_    |
| The Bottleneck    | _Everyone Slows Here_               |
| The Side Exit     | _Unguarded, Unlikely, Known to Few_ |
| The Open Space    | _Nowhere Left to Hide_              |

**Scene Aspects**

- _The Crowd Does Not Know It Is Helping_
- _Authority Cuts Through People Like a Blade_

**Hazards**

- _The Bottleneck_ – anyone passing through in a hurry must overcome at +2 with Athletics or Stealth; failure creates a commotion that gives pursuers a free boost
- _Authority in the Open Space_ – any player character who enters the Open Space is immediately visible to all pursuers; escaping from this zone requires a successful overcome at +4

**Complication** Someone in the crowd recognizes one of the players – and reacts loudly.

## Social Scenes

---

### The Formal Gathering

_A party, ceremony, or official event. Everyone is performing. Information moves sideways. Direct confrontation is the worst possible move._

**Zones**

|Zone|Aspect|
|---|---|
|The Main Floor|_On Display, Under Scrutiny_|
|The Periphery|_Watching Without Being Watched_|
|The Private Alcove|_Conversation That Isn't For Everyone_|
|The Service Area|_Invisible to Guests, Overheard by Staff_|
|The Exit Corridor|_The Last Place to Catch Someone Alone_|

**Scene Aspects**

- _Appearances Are the Only Currency Here_
- _Everyone Is Here for a Reason They Won't State_

**Hazards**

- _Social Exposure_ – any action that would be acceptable elsewhere (direct accusation, physical confrontation, overt surveillance) triggers immediate social consequence; the acting character gains the aspect _Made a Scene_ with two free invocations for their opponents
- _The Watched Conversation_ – any conversation in the Main Floor is potentially overheard; the GM may at any time reveal that someone was listening, with consequences proportional to what was said

**Complication** Two guests have a history that surfaces mid-event and reshapes the social geometry of the room.

---

### The Negotiation Table

_Two or more parties, a specific thing to resolve, and everyone trying to leave with more than they arrived with._

**Zones**

|Zone|Aspect|
|---|---|
|The Table|_Equal Ground, Unequal Power_|
|The Observing Corner|_Advisors, Witnesses, or Threats_|
|The Host's Space|_Their Room, Their Rules_|
|The Doorway|_The Exit Is Always an Argument_|

**Scene Aspects**

- _Whoever Controls the Terms Controls the Outcome_
- _Walking Away Is Always an Option and Everyone Knows It_

**Hazards**

- _The Observing Corner_ – anyone in this zone may make a Notice roll at +2 each exchange; on success, they learn one thing their side's negotiator does not know about the other party's position
- _Breaking the Frame_ – if any character attempts physical action or overt deception during the negotiation, all social rolls for their side take –2 for the remainder of the scene as trust collapses

**Complication** New information arrives mid-negotiation that changes what one party wants or what they're willing to offer.

---

### The Interrogation

_One party has information. Another wants it. The room and the power dynamic are both a weapon._

**Zones**

|Zone|Aspect|
|---|---|
|The Hot Seat|_Exposed, Observed, Pressured_|
|The Control Position|_Standing Over, Looking Down_|
|The Observation Side|_Watching Without Participating_|
|The Door|_Freedom or Threat, Depending on Who Controls It_|

**Scene Aspects**

- _The Room Was Designed for This_
- _Everyone in Here Knows How This Usually Ends_

**Hazards**

- _Psychological Pressure_ – at the end of each exchange in which the subject has not revealed anything, they must overcome with Will at +2 or gain a fragile aspect reflecting their current stress (_Rattled_, _Starting to Slip_, _One Push Away_)
- _The Observation Side_ – anyone watching from here may pass a note, signal, or instruction to the interrogator once per exchange as a free action; if the subject notices (Notice at +3), it resets the pressure dynamic

**Complication** The interrogator learns something mid-scene that changes whether they actually want the truth.

## Stealth & Infiltration Scenes

---

### The Guarded Interior

_A secured space with active patrols, locked areas, and a goal somewhere inside. Time and noise are both enemies._

**Zones**

|Zone|Aspect|
|---|---|
|The Entry Point|_First In, Already Committed_|
|The Patrol Route|_Predictable Until It Isn't_|
|The Secured Room|_What You Came For Is In Here_|
|The Blind Spot|_The Gap in Their Coverage_|
|The Alarm Junction|_One Switch Away from Everything Going Wrong_|

**Scene Aspects**

- _Security Has Layers for a Reason_
- _The Silence Is the Warning System_

**Hazards**

- _Active Patrol_ – at the end of each exchange, roll Notice at +3 for the patrol; on success, a player character in an unsecured zone must immediately make a Stealth overcome at +3 or be spotted
- _The Alarm Junction_ – any failed roll in an adjacent zone has a chance of triggering; the GM may spend a fate point to trigger it regardless of the roll result

**Complication** Something inside the Secured Room is not what was expected – more guarded, different in nature, or already missing.

---

### The Moving Target

_The objective is a person, not a place – and they're in motion through a changing environment._

**Zones**

|Zone|Aspect|
|---|---|
|The Target's Current Position|_Close Enough to Act, Dangerous to Linger_|
|One Step Behind|_In Range, Not Yet Committed_|
|The Approach Route|_The Path They're Taking_|
|The Intercept Point|_Where the Routes Converge_|
|The Public Exposure|_Too Many Eyes for Anything Clean_|

**Scene Aspects**

- _The Window Is Measured in Steps, Not Minutes_
- _The Target Doesn't Know They're Being Followed – Yet_

**Hazards**

- _Detection Threshold_ – each exchange in which the players act in the Target's zone, roll their Stealth against the target's Notice; after two failed rolls, the target is aware and the scene changes character entirely
- _The Public Exposure_ – any action other than observation taken here creates a complication; the GM places a new scene aspect reflecting the public nature of the event

**Complication** Someone else is also following the target, for different reasons, and notices the players first.

## Exploration & Environment Scenes

---

### The Vertical Climb

_Height is the challenge. The goal is above. The ground is a consequence, not a destination._

**Zones**

|Zone|Aspect|
|---|---|
|The Base|_Safe Ground, Nowhere Near the Goal_|
|The Lower Ascent|_Hard Going, Solid Holds_|
|The Exposed Face|_Wind, Visibility, No Shelter_|
|The Crux|_The Hardest Move, the Narrowest Margin_|
|The Summit Approach|_Almost There, Most Dangerous Now_|

**Scene Aspects**

- _The Fall Is Always Present_
- _Height Changes What You Can See_

**Hazards**

- _The Exposed Face_ – each exchange spent here, all characters must overcome with Athletics or Physique at +2 against wind, cold, or exposure; failure adds a mild physical consequence or a fragile aspect (_Fingers Numb_, _Grip Slipping_)
- _The Crux_ – passing through requires an Athletics overcome at +4; failure means falling back one zone and gaining the aspect _Shaken Confidence_ or taking a mild physical consequence; dramatic failure means two zones back

**Complication** Something at the top is not waiting passively – it is aware of the approach and has time to prepare.

---

### The Unstable Environment

_The location itself is failing. Flood, fire, collapse, contamination. The scene has a clock and the clock is structural._

**Zones**

|Zone|Aspect|
|---|---|
|The Safe Pocket|_Still Intact, Won't Be for Long_|
|The Failing Structure|_Everything Here Is Coming Down_|
|The Hazard Core|_The Source of What's Killing This Place_|
|The Escape Route|_The Way Out, Increasingly Blocked_|
|The Trapped Depth|_Furthest from Safety, Closest to What Matters_|

**Scene Aspects**

- _The Building Has Already Made Its Decision_
- _Saving One Thing Means Abandoning Another_

**Hazards**

- _Structural Deterioration_ – at the end of each exchange, the GM moves one zone aspect one step toward collapse; aspects that collapse become hazards that attack anyone in that zone at +3
- _The Hazard Core_ – anyone entering this zone takes a mild physical consequence automatically from the environment; a second exchange here requires a Will overcome at +2 to remain functional

**Complication** Someone who should have left hasn't. They are in the Trapped Depth and are not coming out on their own.

---

### The Wilderness Crossing

_Open country, bad terrain, distance as the obstacle. The environment is indifferent and that's worse than hostile._

**Zones**

|Zone|Aspect|
|---|---|
|The Known Edge|_Mapped, Familiar, Already Behind You_|
|The Open Stretch|_Exposed and Slow_|
|The Difficult Ground|_Marsh, Rock, Dense Growth – Pick Your Suffering_|
|The Landmark|_Navigation Point, Possible Shelter, Possible Trap_|
|The Threshold|_The Far Side, If You Reach It_|

**Scene Aspects**

- _The Land Does Not Care About Your Schedule_
- _What You Carry Determines What You Can Do_

**Hazards**

- _Exposure_ – for each exchange (or scene, for slower pacing) spent in the Open Stretch without shelter or cover, characters must overcome with Physique at +2 or gain a mild physical consequence from weather, exhaustion, or terrain
- _The Difficult Ground_ – movement through this zone costs a full action and an Athletics overcome at +2; failure means no progress and the aspect _Spent More Than Expected_

**Complication** Something is tracking the group across the crossing. It is patient, it knows the terrain, and it has been following since the Known Edge.

## Mixed & Hybrid Scenes

---

### The Crowd in Crisis

_A large group of people in a dangerous situation. The players must act while the environment and the crowd both generate pressure._

**Zones**

|Zone|Aspect|
|---|---|
|The Crush|_Mass Panic, No One in Control_|
|The Clearing|_Temporary Space, Won't Stay Empty_|
|The High Point|_Visible to Everyone, Target for Everything_|
|The Exit Funnel|_The Only Way Out for Most People_|
|The Source|_Where Whatever Started This Is Coming From_|

**Scene Aspects**

- _Fear Moves Faster Than People_
- _Heroes and Obstacles Look the Same in a Crowd_

**Hazards**

- _The Crush_ – anyone in this zone at the end of an exchange must overcome with Physique or Athletics at +2 or be moved one zone involuntarily by crowd pressure
- _The Exit Funnel_ – each exchange, one additional civilian is added to this zone; after three exchanges, it becomes impassable without a +4 overcome; after five, someone is hurt regardless

**Complication** The source of the crisis is not what it appears to be – and addressing the apparent cause makes the real one worse.

---

### The Split Objective

_Two things need doing at the same time in different places. The players cannot be everywhere._

**Zones**

|Zone|Aspect|
|---|---|
|Site Alpha|_First Priority, First Pressure_|
|Site Beta|_Second Priority, Already in Trouble_|
|The Distance Between|_Every Step Here Is a Step Away from Both_|
|The Convergence|_Where Both Problems Are Heading_|
|The Unexpected Third|_Nobody Planned for This_|

**Scene Aspects**

- _Splitting Up Solves One Problem and Creates Two_
- _The Clock Does Not Wait for Consensus_

**Hazards**

- _Divergence Pressure_ – at the end of each exchange, the GM advances whichever site has fewer players present one step toward failure; after three steps, that objective is lost
- _The Unexpected Third_ – once per scene the GM may introduce a development in a zone no player is currently in; it requires immediate attention or becomes a scene aspect with two free invocations for the opposition

**Complication** The two objectives turn out to be connected in a way that means solving both the obvious way makes the underlying problem worse.
